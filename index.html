1 · Register

Entry is KSh 100 per manager. Admin writes the confirmed participants and tracks the collected entry pot.

2 · Play the groups

The tournament runs over the gameweeks Admin sets. Admin enters each manager’s group record and combined FPL points for those gameweeks. Group tables sort by group points, goal difference, goals scored, then name.

3 · Advance to knockouts

Admin decides and records who advances, then enters each Round of 16, quarter-final, semi-final and final fixture and score. The bracket groups matches by round and highlights each recorded winner.

4 · Prize winner

The manager with the highest combined FPL points across the tournament gameweeks wins the KSh 100-per-entry pot and is shown with the cup. A points tie is decided by group points, goal difference, goals scored, then manager name. The knockout bracket tracks progression; it does not decide the cash prize.

Bench Swapping

An optional, admin-reviewed substitution request.

How Bench Swapping works

  1. Send your manager name, bench player and starting player with the reason.
  2. The request fee is KSh 30; Admin confirms payment before making an accepted swap.
  3. The bench player must have more points than the starting player.
  4. The starting player must have a red card, an injury, or zero points while being your captain.
  5. Admin reviews the request against these rules and confirms the outcome.
Late contribution penalty: Pay by the countdown deadline. A KSh 30 penalty applies to a contribution recorded after it. Admin checks payment time and marks the payment status.
